### Parametric by design

Every object can be driven by properties, constraints, expressions, and other objects. Changes are recorded in a precise, editable history.

{{< /group >}}

{{< group >}}

### Open formats

Import and export STEP, IGES, OBJ, STL, DXF, SVG, IFC, and many other formats, with FreeCAD's native FCStd format for your editable models.

{{< /group >}}

{{< group >}}

### No lock-in

FreeCAD is community-developed and available across Windows, macOS, and GNU/Linux. Your software, files, and workflow remain yours.

{{< /group >}}
