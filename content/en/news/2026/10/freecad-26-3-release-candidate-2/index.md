Hot on the heels of the first release candidate last week we are pleased to announce that builds for [26.3 Release Candidate 2 (RC2) are now available](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/releases/tag/26.3rc2). RC2 contains fixes from RC1 including those blockers that were reported by you (brilliant) users who tested it.

We would love it if you download RC2 and test again, if you find a problem please search to see if it is previously reported, and, if not, do [raise an issue](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/issues) helping us to make 26.3 an excellent release.
