The FPA members have voted to fund three projects in the third quarter.

**Morten Vajhøj** will work on [topological naming and feature identification in TechDraw](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FPA-grant-proposals/issues/108). Morten will focus on four deliverables: HLR toponaming, attaching callouts to geometry rather the view location, automatic feature identification, and a dozen new features, such as automatic BOM and balloons in assemblies, and automatic hole/thread callouts. Morten joined the project in early 2026, his [contributions](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/pulls?q=is%3Apr+state%3Aclosed+author%3AMortenVajhoj) are all in/around TechDraw and the Measurement tool. He is one of our GSoC students this year, working on a post-selection rewrite of TechDraw. Morten will be awarded a grant of EUR 2,000 upon the successful completion of the project.

**João Matos** will be improving the [visual appeal of rendering in the 3D view](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FPA-grant-proposals/issues/107). Deliverables are: multi-pass infrastructure, linear HDR rendering and correct color handling, physically based material shading, image-based/environment lighting, real-time ambient occlusion, real-time directional-light shadows, and more. João's previous work involved modernizing FreeCAD's and Coin3D's rendering architecture. He will be awarded a grant of EUR 4,000 once the project is completed.

**Amritpal Singh** will work on [KiCad integration for Ondsel Lens](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FPA-grant-proposals/issues/110). Expected deliverables include 3D board viewing, dual ECAD/MCAD viewers, and STEP export. This work is funded from a [separate Ondsel Onwards fund](/news/the-ondsel-onwards-fund/) submitted by a private sponsor. Amritpal will be awarded a grant of EUR 3,360 when he completes the project.

The total funds allocation is EUR 9,360 against the EUR 10,000 budget.
