Once again the exciting prospect of [FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/2027/) (ULB Brussels Jan 30th 31st 2027) draws ever nearer. It’s such an amazing chance for the broad open source and open culture communities to draw together and share stories, ideas and expertise.

Once again Chris Hennes (chennes) and myself (concretedog) are managing the Open Hardware CAD/CAM dev room and are delighted to announce the call for participation is now open. We want to see as broad a range of applications to curate a great room for everyone so are interested to hear from you if you are building open hardware projects or working on open hardware tooling, CAD/CAM and beyond.

More detail can be [found on this page](https://openhardwaredevroom.github.io/FOSDEM2027/) with the link to the CFP form towards the bottom of the text. One thing of note is that if you submit an application you need to double check that you select the Open Hardware CAD/CAM room option from the drop down menu on the form, or your excellent talk might end up somewhere else!
