Hot on the heals of version 1.1.0 we are pleased to [announce the release of version 1.1.1.](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/releases/tag/1.1.1)

You can check out the release notes and the full change log on the repository, it's fair to say that there are a LOT of small fixes to enhance your experiences with FreeCAD.

As ever massive thanks to all contributors and developers for taking the time and effort to continue to improve FreeCAD. The website download section will update soon (Edit: the [website download section is now up to date](https://www.freecad.org/downloads.php?lang=en)) but for now you can download 1.1.1 packages from the ["Assets" section at the bottom of the release page.](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/releases/tag/1.1.1)
