We are excited to announce that the 2026 North American FreeCAD Meetup will be happening August the 7th through 9th in Knoxville Tennessee.

Hosted by [Knox Makers™](https://knoxmakers.org/) this informal gathering will include ad hoc presentations, discussion, tutorials and more. We hope to see a range of the FreeCAD community there, from developers and maintainers, to expert users and those new to FreeCAD. All are welcome.

Register now over [on the event page](https://events.freecad.org/conferences/freecad-north-america-2026) and we look forward to seeing you there.
