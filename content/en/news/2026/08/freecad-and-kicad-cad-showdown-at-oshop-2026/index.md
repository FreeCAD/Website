After the success of the [E-sport style FreeCAD and KiCad speed run competitions recently held at EMF camp](/news/freecad-e-sport-speed-run-challenge-at-emf-camp-2026/), we are delighted to spot that the team behind this event are hosting another! If you are around [OSHOP, the open source hardware conference](https://jtc.uni-halle.de/en/event/oshop/) running in the [Just Transition Centre](https://jtc.uni-halle.de/en/about-us/), Halle (Saale), Germany on the 23rd and 24th of September [do sign up to take part via this pad](https://md.ccc.ac/OSHOP2026-CAD-Showdown?both#).

There are example challenges to try out on the link, then at the event the small challenges will be released as each round of this friendly and informal competition runs. The EMF camp event was great fun with a good energy in the room, definitely have a go no matter what your FreeCAD or KiCad experience level. Also, even if you don't want to take part, go along and cheer on the competitors!
