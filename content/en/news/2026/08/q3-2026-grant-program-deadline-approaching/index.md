This is a quick reminder that the submission deadline for Q3 2026 grant applications is September 1. If you are interested in improving FreeCAD and its ecosystem, please [read more](https://fpa.freecad.org/programs/fpadf-announcement) about the program and [apply](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FPA-grant-proposals/issues).

Please keep in mind that typically there are multiple grant requests every time, and the quarterly budget for the program is 10K euro.

On a related note, Morgan Allen recently completed his [grant work](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FPA-grant-proposals/issues/72) and released [KiConnect](https://codeberg.org/kiconnect/KiConnect) 1.0.0, and addon that implements direct data exchange with KiCad so you can pull an updated PCB design into your FreeCAD project while having both applications open. The addon is available in the Addon Manager. You can also read Morgan's [blog post](https://oit.cloud/posts/2026-07-16-kiconnect_released/) about the release.
