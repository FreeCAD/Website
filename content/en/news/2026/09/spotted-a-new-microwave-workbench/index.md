Over on our FreeCAD Mastodon account, under our regular #FreeCADFriday hashtag we get to spot all manner of things. Often people post that weeks little 3D printing project, or a small technical drawing, or an update on a longer FreeCAD project they are undertaking. Sometimes though people pop in with amazing new bits of functionality they are building for FreeCAD.

Doing just that Mike posted, and continues to post, about their development of the [Microwave Workbench](https://github.com/mishka-zz/freecad-microwave), chock full of tools for high frequency design and simulation.

The author of this post can't pretend to understand masses of it so it's probably for the best if readers dive over to the project repository and take a look through the detailed readme. The readme includes lists of all the solvers, the types of simulation result you can achieve as well as the modelling capabilities this workbench can perform.

Not in the addon manager yet, the Microwave workbench readme again has great instructions for set up and there are examples of both models and analysis projects in the repository for you to get going.

It's also definitely worth following [Mike on the fediverse](https://mathstodon.xyz/@mishkathebear) as they are regularly posting about development there. As for me, I'm going to go and research "Eigenmodes"!
