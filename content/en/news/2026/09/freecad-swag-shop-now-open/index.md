We are super pleased to announce that the [FreeCAD official swag store is now open](https://shop.freecad.org/en-gbp)! All the designs in there are made in FreeCAD and we have tee, hoodies, mugs, water bottles and more. Feel free to take a look.

![](swag2.webp)

If you do make a purchase know that a small donation is made to the [FPA](https://fpa.freecad.org/) from each item. If you are feeling even more generous there are options at checkout to increase your donation amount to support our work.
