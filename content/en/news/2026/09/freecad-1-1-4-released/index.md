The FreeCAD development team has just released v1.1.4 that fixes over 30 bugs in the stable v1.1 series. There are no new features in this release (expect those in v26.3 to be released later this year).

The majority of patches fix issues in TechDraw, BIM, and the user interface. The most notable fixes include a fix for a segfault in Part when opening files with faulty geometry and safeguards against [ZipSlip vulnerabilities](https://security.snyk.io/research/zip-slip-vulnerability).

You can find [download links](https://www.freecad.org/downloads.php) for Linux, Windows, and macOS on the main website.
