We are pleased to announce the release of version 1.1.2. This release pulls important fixes from the current development branch without adding any new features.

32% of the backported patches fix issues in BIM/Arch and the core. Another 12% are security fixes, so we do recommend [downloading](https://www.freecad.org/downloads.php?lang=en) and installing the new version. For the full list of changes, please see the [GitHub release page](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/releases/tag/1.1.2).

We thank all contributors who were part of the effort: developers, maintainers, translators, testers, and community members who provided vital feedback.
