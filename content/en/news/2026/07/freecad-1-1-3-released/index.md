Hot on the heels of v1.1.2, FreeCAD 1.1.3 is out to include a patch we forgot backporting. The new version includes a fix for repeated version update warnings when saving files. There are no security risks or crashes involved, but the warnings are annoying, so you will likely want to [upgrade](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/releases/tag/1.1.3)!
