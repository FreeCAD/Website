Just in time for your weekend FreeCAD adventures, we are proud to announce that [FreeCAD 26.3 RC3 builds are now available](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/releases/tag/26.3rc3) for download and testing. Excitingly there are no open blocker issues currently for RC3 and if that continues to be the case then we can expect the next stable release to follow in the next few weeks.

As ever, we’d love it if you could download, test and report your findings.
