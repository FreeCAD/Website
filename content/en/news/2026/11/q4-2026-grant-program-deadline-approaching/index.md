A quick reminder to contributors interested in applying for a grant from the FreeCAD Project Association: the last day to submit your application is **November 15**. Voting should be complete by December 19.

Interested? Please [read more](https://fpa.freecad.org/programs/fpadf-announcement) about the program and [apply](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FPA-grant-proposals/issues).

We tend to have multiple grant requests every time, our quarterly budget is 10K euros, and we also pay reverse VAT, so we ask you to budget your work with that in mind.
