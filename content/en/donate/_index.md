### Donations Frequently Asked Questions

{{< details summary="Why donate to FreeCAD?" name="faq" >}}

FreeCAD is run entirely by community members. All donations go to the non-profit [FreeCAD Project Association AISBL](https://fpa.freecad.org/) (FPA), which are then used for its [Initiatives](https://fpa.freecad.org/programs.html), including but not limited to:

- Development grants
- Travel grants
- Bug bounty payouts
- Infrastructure

{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How is the money spent?" name="faq" >}}

Check out the [FPA Bookkeeping](https://fpa.freecad.org/handbook/process/bookkeeping/overview.html) to see how the resources are allocated.

{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How to donate?" name="faq" >}}

It is possible to commit to monthly donations called *sponsorship* or make one-time donations using the payment methods provided by the form on the top of this page. Monthly *sponsorship* grants a sponsor level depending on the amount.

The FPA accepts donations from individuals and corporations alike. Donations solely aimed at placing companies' links on the Donate page are not accepted. Sponsors must have some form of relationship with the FreeCAD project.

For more details, visit [Accepting donations](https://fpa.freecad.org/handbook/process/accepting-donations.html).

{{< /details >}}

{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Other ways to support" name="faq" >}}

As a open and community project, everyone can get involved with FreeCAD: contributions such as bug reports, testing, translations, documentation, development, outreach, and many more are always welcome.

FreeCAD swag can also be bought via the [official shop](shop). Each purchase contains a small €/$ 2.00 donation to the FPA, with the possibility to increase this amount if desired.

{{< /details >}}
