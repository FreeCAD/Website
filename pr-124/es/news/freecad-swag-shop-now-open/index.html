FreeCAD Swag Shop Now Open!

11 de septiembre de 2026 Jo Hinchliffe (99)Announcement (115)Community (21)FPA (52)1 min

We are super pleased to announce that the FreeCAD official swag store is now open! All the designs in there are made in FreeCAD and we have tee, hoodies, mugs, water bottles and more. Feel free to take a look.

If you do make a purchase know that a small donation is made to the FPA from each item. If you are feeling even more generous there are options at checkout to increase your donation amount to support our work.
