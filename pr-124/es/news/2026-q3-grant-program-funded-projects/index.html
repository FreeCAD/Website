2026 Q3 grant program - funded projects

2 de octubre de 2026 Aleksandr Prokudin (215)Announcement (115)FPA (52)Grant (27)2 min

The FPA members have voted to fund three projects in the third quarter.

Morten Vajhøj will work on topological naming and feature identification in TechDraw. Morten will focus on four deliverables: HLR toponaming, attaching callouts to geometry rather the view location, automatic feature identification, and a dozen new features, such as automatic BOM and balloons in assemblies, and automatic hole/thread callouts. Morten joined the project in early 2026, his contributions are all in/around TechDraw and the Measurement tool. He is one of our GSoC students this year, working on a post-selection rewrite of TechDraw. Morten will be awarded a grant of EUR 2,000 upon the successful completion of the project.

João Matos will be improving the visual appeal of rendering in the 3D view. Deliverables are: multi-pass infrastructure, linear HDR rendering and correct color handling, physically based material shading, image-based/environment lighting, real-time ambient occlusion, real-time directional-light shadows, and more. João’s previous work involved modernizing FreeCAD’s and Coin3D’s rendering architecture. He will be awarded a grant of EUR 4,000 once the project is completed.

Amritpal Singh will work on KiCad integration for Ondsel Lens. Expected deliverables include 3D board viewing, dual ECAD/MCAD viewers, and STEP export. This work is funded from a separate Ondsel Onwards fund submitted by a private sponsor. Amritpal will be awarded a grant of EUR 3,360 when he completes the project.

The total funds allocation is EUR 9,360 against the EUR 10,000 budget.
