Register for North America FreeCAD Meetup!

2026年6月8日 Jo Hinchliffe (99)Announcement (115)Community (21)Meetup (2)1 分钟

We are excited to announce that the 2026 North American FreeCAD Meetup will be happening August the 7th through 9th in Knoxville Tennessee.

Hosted by Knox Makers™ this informal gathering will include ad hoc presentations, discussion, tutorials and more. We hope to see a range of the FreeCAD community there, from developers and maintainers, to expert users and those new to FreeCAD. All are welcome.

Register now over on the event page and we look forward to seeing you there.
