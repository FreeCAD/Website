Built for the way ideas evolve

Parametric

Change the design, not the whole model

Build with constraints, dimensions, and a modelling history. Update an earlier decision and let dependent geometry follow.

Interoperable

Keep control of your work

Work with STEP, IFC, STL, DXF, SVG, and many other formats. Your native project files stay editable and yours.

Extensible

Shape the tool around your workflow

Use dedicated workbenches, install community addons, automate tasks with Python, or create tools of your own.
