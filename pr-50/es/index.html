Why FreeCAD?

Parametric and editable

Build with constraints, expressions, and a clear modeling history that stays editable as designs change.

Open formats, no lock-in

Import and export STEP, IFC, STL, DXF, SVG, and more. Keep native FCStd files and move projects between tools.

Cross-platform and extensible

Run FreeCAD on Windows, macOS, or GNU/Linux, then extend it with Python, macros, and addons.

A workflow for every kind of making

Product design and manufacture

Create editable solids, assemblies, technical drawings, and toolpaths for CNC workflows. Revise a design without starting over.

Explorar→

Architecture and BIM

Work with building information, quantities, and documentation using dedicated BIM and Draft workbenches.

Explorar→

Sketch, print, and automate

Prepare models for 3D printing, run finite-element analyses, or extend FreeCAD with Python, macros, and community workbenches.
